Feature guide

How do I import a score?

Open Score, choose MusicXML Editor for existing MusicXML, or Scan to MusicXML for PDF/image sources. Scanned results should be reviewed before practice.

How do I edit MusicXML?

Select a symbol in the score editor, adjust it in the inspector, and save the corrected score. The corrected MusicXML becomes the source for playback and practice.

How do I practice?

Choose a score, select the part you want to rehearse, and use playback, mixer, metronome, pitch reference, and practice review tools.

How do I get help?

Visit www.vocaldive.com or email support@vocaldive.com.
